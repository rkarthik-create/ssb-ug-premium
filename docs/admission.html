Eligibility

Who can apply.

Class XII

Students currently in Class XII, or who passed Class XII in 2026.

60% minimum

Minimum 60% in Class XII from a recognised board (CBSE, ISC, state boards, IB, IGCSE or equivalent).

All streams

Open to science, commerce and humanities.

Admission process

Five steps from information session to offer.

1Information session

Register and book a 30-minute live session with the admissions team. Attendance is mandatory to proceed.

2SSAT

A 70-minute online proctored test within 7 days of the session. JEE, SAT, CUET or IPMAT scorers can skip it.

3Application

Academic records, up to five extracurriculars, the ₹1,000 application fee and a video essay within 3 days.

4Interviews

An online AI-led interview, then a case interview with senior leadership at the Bengaluru campus.

5Decision

Results within 10 days. Confirm your seat within 10 days of the offer by paying the ₹50,000 admission fee.
